Common signs

Is your foundation moving?

  • Doors or windows that stick or won't latch
  • Stair-step cracks in brick or mortar
  • Diagonal drywall cracks above doors and windows
  • Gaps between the baseboard and the floor
  • Sloping or bouncy floors
  • Water pooling against the slab after rain
