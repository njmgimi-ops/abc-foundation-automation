🚨 Foundation emergency?

We answer 24/7. For the fastest help, call. Our assistant connects urgent calls to the on-call manager.

📞 Call now
If you smell gas, see sparking wires, or a wall or ceiling is actively moving: leave the house and call 911 first. Then call your gas or electric utility. We'll help once everyone is safe.

What counts as urgent

  • Water coming up through the slab or flooding near the foundation
  • A new crack that is widening quickly
  • Doors or windows that suddenly won't open (possible egress risk)
  • A wall visibly separating from the house

Everything else, like slow cracks or sticking doors, is best handled by a normal free inspection.
