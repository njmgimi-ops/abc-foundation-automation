Send us photos before your inspection

Photos help your inspector bring the right equipment and give you a more accurate estimate on the first visit.

📸 Photo tips

  • Put a coin or tape measure next to the crack for scale
  • One wide shot (whole wall) plus one close-up
  • Doors: a photo of the gap at the top corner when closed
  • Videos: walk slowly and say which room you're in

Files go to a private folder for your job and are only used for your inspection.
